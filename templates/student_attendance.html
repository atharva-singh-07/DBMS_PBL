Official Attendance Counting Policy

• Formula: Attendance Percentage = (Number of Present Classes / Total Counted Classes) × 100.
• Total Counted Classes: Encompasses all held regular, extra, and laboratory sessions marked as Present, Absent, or Leave.
• Approved Leave: Approved leaves are formally excused and reflected under Leave records; faculty can take them into account for shortage condonation.
• Makeup Sessions: Participation in scheduled makeup classes provides instructional recovery and is tracked in the Makeup Attendance register.
